## Opening your profile

Your profile opens from the user menu (your avatar, at the top right): <Ui>Profile & usage limits</Ui> slides out a panel on the right of the screen, in the dashboard or on any public page. It closes with the × in the corner, with <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> or with a click outside it.

<Screenshot name="profile-sheet" alt="The profile panel open: the photo, the name, the email and the role; the form to edit the display name, the usage limits and the danger zone with the Deactivate account button." />

At the top are your photo (your Google account's, or your initials if it has none), your name, your email and your role.

## Editing your name

<Ui>Edit display name</Ui> changes the name you appear with on the platform, for example in the user menu. At first it is your Google account's name.

1. Type the new name in the <Ui>Display name</Ui> field (up to 255 characters).
2. Click <Ui>Save</Ui>. The button is only enabled if the name has changed.
3. A notice confirms the change: <Ui>Display name updated.</Ui>

The change only affects SiLEnSeSS: your Google account does not change.

## Your usage limits

<Ui>Usage limits</Ui> shows the quotas of your current role, the same ones the wizard validates every simulation against:

- <Ui>Agents</Ui>: the maximum number of agents per network.
- <Ui>Iterations</Ui>: the maximum number of rounds (the iteration limit).
- <Ui>Networks</Ui>: the maximum number of networks per run.
- <Ui>Density factor</Ui>: the factor the server multiplies a generated network's density by.

An <Ui>Unlimited</Ui> value has no cap. The limits depend on the role, not on what you have already launched: they do not run out with use. The table of every role is in [Getting started](/docs/getting-started#roles-and-quotas); to change role, write to the administrators (see [Contact](/contact)).

## Deactivating the account

The <Ui>Danger zone</Ui>, at the end of the panel, lets you deactivate your account.

<Warning>
<Ui>Deactivate account</Ui> deactivates your account permanently: it cannot be undone from the platform. Once you confirm, your session is closed immediately and the platform stops accepting your account; only an administrator can reactivate it.
</Warning>

1. Click <Ui>Deactivate account</Ui>.
2. The browser asks for confirmation: <Ui>Are you sure you want to deactivate your account? You will be logged out immediately.</Ui>
3. If you accept, the button shows <Ui>Deactivating…</Ui> and then your session is closed.

If you only want to leave, use <Ui>Sign out</Ui> in the user menu: your account stays active.
